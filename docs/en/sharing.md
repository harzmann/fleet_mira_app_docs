# Export, email and backup

## Export an original

Open **Verlauf**, expand the session and choose **Exportieren** for a complete file. Android's file picker selects the destination. The original stays in the app. Check that the exported copy exists and is complete at the chosen destination.

Export backs up the selected original, not automatically profiles, credentials, all history entries or the entire app storage.

## Send to a server

**Senden** opens target selection. Choose an active configured target. The durable job may wait for Wi-Fi or the end of a USB session. History shows whether only receipt or the final destination/import state has been confirmed. For **Korrektur erforderlich**, correct the displayed cause and retry.

## Hand files to an email app

1. Open **Einstellungen → Anhänge auswählen**, or the email action in an expanded session.
2. Select one or more complete files. Count and total size are displayed.
3. Choose **An Mail-App übergeben**.
4. Select a suitable email app in Android. Check recipients and attachments and decide whether to send there.

A handoff supports at most 100 attachments; the email app may impose lower size/count limits. Fleet Mira App does not use its own SMTP connection. A handoff or entry under **Letzte Übergaben** is not proof of sending or delivery. Cancelling the Android dialog sends nothing.

## Before replacing a device or uninstalling

Export or transfer all required complete originals. Independently verify copies or confirmed archive import at the destination. Record required settings according to your organisation's policy; obtain new-device access from the administrator. Android cloud backup is disabled. Do not clear app storage before verifying your backup.
